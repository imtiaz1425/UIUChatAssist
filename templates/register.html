Terms of Service

1. Acceptable Use: UIU ChatAssist is an AI campus guide intended for United International University students, faculty, and administrative staff to query university information.

2. Academic Integrity: The assistant provides guidance on publicly available UIU academic policies, courses, schedules, and faculty contacts. It must not be used for malicious activities or unauthorized system probing.

3. Informational Purpose: All answers are generated using official university portal information. For formal administrative decisions, always verify with the relevant UIU department or Registrar's office.

Privacy Policy

1. Data Collection: We collect your full name, student university ID, and official university email solely to authenticate access and personalize your campus experience.

2. Security: Passwords are cryptographically hashed using standard salted hashes before storage. Plaintext passwords are never saved or exposed.

3. Chat Records: Conversation logs are stored securely in the local campus database to allow you to review previous chats. Your data is not sold or shared with third parties.
